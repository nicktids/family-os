# Auto-approval

Auto-approval skips the waiting for routine suggestions. Open **Settings → AI → Auto-approval**. Only admins see it.

## What it does

Some suggestions are routine: a shopping item, a task. For these you can turn auto-approval on. When the AI suggests one for a person who has the right you choose, it is applied within moments, as if you had approved it yourself. That covers what the AI makes from something shared to Quick add, in the [assistant](assistant.md), or through an agent they connected ([MCP](mcp.md)). Two kinds can be approved this way:

- **Shopping items.** Suggestions to add shopping items. You choose who the suggestion must be for: someone who can **Create, rename and remove shopping lists**, or, more widely, someone who can **Add and tick off shopping items**.
- **Tasks.** The same for tasks, for someone who can **Add and change tasks for anyone**.

Turn on the switch for the kind, then pick the right under **Apply the AI's suggestions when they are for someone who can**. Suggestions for anyone else still wait for a person. Only these two kinds exist; everything else always waits for a person.

## Only the AI's suggestions

People who hold these rights already add shopping items and tasks themselves, without a suggestion. So the suggestions that wait are the ones the AI makes for them. Turning the switch on lets those through without anyone seeing them first, so do it only for routine you trust, such as shopping. The AI may have read a forwarded message with hidden instructions in it, which is why this is a choice and not the default.

## What it does not do

- It approves **as the person who turned it on**. It can do nothing that person could not do by hand, and the usual rules for approving apply.
- A suggestion addressed to one person alone (a note the assistant offers to remember) is always theirs to decide.
- It does not touch people, roles or settings: those suggestions never qualify.

## What you see

An approved suggestion stays in Quick add, marked **Approved automatically**. You can still **Undo** it there, like any approval. The same goes for a suggestion in the assistant's answer. If the AI added it, check Quick add now and then.

## When it stops

Auto-approval is checked each time a suggestion comes in. If the person who turned it on is archived, or may no longer manage auto-approval or approve that kind of suggestion, it **stops by itself** and sends a notification, **Auto-approval stopped**. It goes to that person, or to the other admins if that person is gone. Suggestions wait for a person again. Turn it on again to continue: it then approves as you.

## Other automation

For anything beyond this, ask the [assistant](assistant.md), or connect your own agent ([MCP](mcp.md)). What they propose arrives as suggestions you approve.
