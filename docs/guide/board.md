# The family board

The **Board** is the family's fridge door: short notes on paper, for everyone. It is the right place for "Dinner is at 7", "The plumber comes Friday morning" or "Wi-Fi password: on the fridge". It is not a chat: there are no replies or threads. Each person can only mark a note as seen, and that happens by itself when they look at the board.

Find it under **Household** in the side bar on a computer, and under **More → Household** on a phone. The first notes also appear on **Today**, in the same order, and on a family display (see [Family displays](displays.md)). The board is as tall as its notes: a few notes make a small board.

## Putting up a note

The first tile on the board says **Write a note…**. Tap it and it turns into the paper you are writing:

- **The note**: up to 500 characters; line breaks are kept. A counter appears when you are close to the limit.
- **Paper**: five papers shown as large swatches; the one you pick is ringed and ticked, and the note you are writing turns that colour. They are only for looks, and you can change the paper later with **Edit**. A note keeps its paper everywhere it appears (Today, a display). The colors come from the theme you use ([Appearance](appearance.md)).
- **Add a photo**: one photo per note. It is shrunk on your device first, and the picture is saved again without its hidden camera details, such as where it was taken.
- **Stays on the board**: **Until I take it down**, **Until today**, **Until tomorrow**, **Until the end of the week** or **Until a day I pick…**. Days follow your family's time zone and week start ([Region and formats](region.md)). A note leaves the board after its last day.
- **Pin it to the top**: only for people who can manage the board (admins by default).

**Put it up** adds the note; **Cancel** throws the draft away. Everyone else who can see the board is told about a new note (the start of its text), except you. Each person can switch these notifications off under **Notifications**.

## Reading the board

Pinned notes sit first, held by a strip of tape. The other notes follow in the order the family arranged them; a new note goes first. Each note shows who wrote it, how long ago, and **Until Fri** or similar when it ends. At its foot a line says who has seen it, in words: "Seen by Ayşe and Ece" (up to three names), or "Seen by 4" when more have. The writer is not counted. Tap a photo to see it large.

## Arranging the notes

Drag a note to where you want it: with a mouse, press and move; on a touch screen, hold the note for a moment, then move it. The note lifts and the others make a gap where it will land. The order is kept for everyone, and Today shows the board's first notes in the same order.

The **…** menu has **Move earlier** and **Move later** too, for a keyboard or a screen reader. Pinned notes always stay first: a note moves among the pinned ones, or among the others, and pinning a note puts it at the top of the pinned ones. You can move your own notes; a manager (admins by default) can move any note.

## Changing a note

The **…** button on a note opens its actions:

- **Edit**: change the text, the paper, the end day or the photo. Only the person who wrote it, or a manager, can.
- **Move earlier** / **Move later**: one place at a time (see above).
- **Pin** / **Unpin**: managers only.
- **Take down**: tap, then tap again to confirm. The author or a manager can; the photo is deleted with it.

A note whose last day has passed is hidden at once. FamilyOS deletes it, with its photo, 30 days later.

## What the AI does here

If you share something to Quick add that is meant for the whole family, the AI can suggest a note, which a person approves like any suggestion (and can undo). The assistant can read the board when you ask what is on it. To keep the board away from the AI, set **Board** to **Off** under **Settings → AI → What the AI may read**. Nothing is posted without a person approving it.

## Who can do what

| | See | Put up a note, change or take down your own | Pin, take down anyone's note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Admin | yes | yes | yes |
| Member | yes | yes | no |
| Supervised member | yes | yes | no |
| Guest | yes | no | no |
| Family display | yes (if granted) | no | no |

These follow the **Board** permissions of each role (**Settings → Family → Roles**): *See the notes on the board*, *Put up notes, and change or take down your own*, and *Pin notes and take down anyone's note*.

## On a family display

The board widget shows the notes in the board's order (pinned notes first), in their papers and in larger type. There is nothing to touch, and a display never marks a note as seen. Pair the display with the board permission to show it; see [Family displays](displays.md).
